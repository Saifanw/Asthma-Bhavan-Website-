Our Services

Comprehensive pulmonary care under one roof

From everyday allergies to complex lung conditions, our specialists diagnose, treat and manage the full range of respiratory illness. Pick the one that matches your symptoms.

Asthma

Diagnosis and long-term control plans for children and adults, including allergic and exercise-induced asthma.

Common signs: Wheezing, night-time cough, chest tightness, breathlessness.

Allergy & Rhinitis

Allergy testing and personalised treatment for sneezing, blocked nose and allergic triggers.

Common signs: Repeated sneezing, runny or blocked nose, itchy eyes, seasonal flare-ups.

COPD

Care and rehabilitation for chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.

Common signs: Long-standing cough, phlegm, breathlessness while climbing stairs.

Sleep Apnea & Snoring

Evaluation and treatment of obstructive sleep apnea (OSA).

Common signs: Loud snoring, pauses in breathing at night, daytime sleepiness.

Bronchoscopy & EBUS

Advanced diagnostic procedures including thoracoscopy and endobronchial ultrasound.

Common signs: Usually advised by your pulmonologist for persistent cough or unexplained findings on a chest scan.

Pulmonary Fibrosis & ILD

Specialist assessment and management of interstitial lung disease.

Common signs: Dry cough and breathlessness that slowly worsen over months.

Diagnostics & services

  • Pulmonary Function Tests (PFT)
  • FeNO & diffusion capacity testing

Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions

What does Asthma Bhawan treat?

Asthma Bhawan treats asthma, allergy and rhinitis, COPD, sleep apnea and snoring, interstitial lung disease and other respiratory conditions. Diagnostics include pulmonary function tests (PFT), FeNO testing, allergy testing, and bronchoscopy with EBUS.

Where is Asthma Bhawan located?

Asthma Bhawan is at R-3, Sector-6, Vidhyadhar Nagar, Jaipur-302039, Rajasthan.

Is emergency care for breathing problems available at night?

Yes. Asthma Bhawan provides 24/7 emergency respiratory care. The emergency number is +91 141 2235005.

How do I book an appointment?

Call +91 93529 34531, message the team on WhatsApp, or use the form on this page to send your details or request a call back.

Does Asthma Bhawan offer free or subsidized treatment?

Yes. The hospital offers free and subsidized treatment for patients who need help. Please speak to the team to understand what applies to you.

When should I go to emergency instead of waiting for an appointment?

Seek emergency care straight away if there is severe breathlessness, difficulty speaking full sentences, blue or grey lips, or if a reliever inhaler is not helping. This is general guidance and not a substitute for medical advice.

Who founded Asthma Bhawan?

Asthma Bhawan was founded in 2007 by the Indian Asthma Care Society under the leadership of Dr. Virendra Singh, a specialist in allergy and asthma.
